About

Tamaki Technologies LLC is a software development and consulting company. The company has two divisions: Business Software & Consulting and Games & Entertainment.

Business Software & Consulting

This division takes fixed-fee work in pension calculation automation, SQL and data reconciliation, Excel and Python automation, and technical consulting. RetirementEngine.io is the flagship product. It is a separate website, developed by Tamaki Technologies LLC, and it is not hosted on this site.

Games & Entertainment

Our games include card games, board games, and word games designed for quick play, friendly competition, and sharing with friends. We believe a good game shouldn't require a long tutorial or a complicated setup. The goal is to understand the basics quickly and start playing.

Our games are designed to work well on a variety of devices. Some can be played locally on one device, while others can be opened into an online room to play with friends. Each game has its own web address so that players can go directly to the game they want to play.

Tamaki Technologies publishes games for the web and Android, with games also being developed for Apple and iOS. Additional platforms and games are planned as the portfolio grows.

We keep our games straightforward and try to avoid unnecessary barriers to playing. Our games can be played without creating an account, and we aim to collect only the information that is necessary to provide the features of each game.

The current game portfolio is being developed one game at a time. New games and features are added as they are ready.
