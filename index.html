Games

Eight games are playable in the browser now. Each one also has an Android app on Google Play.

  • Ochos LocosMatch the suit, drop an eight, and race to clear your hand.
  • Thirty-OneDraw, discard, and knock. Build a three-card hand as close to 31 as you can.
  • DingusEmpty your hand first and you run the room. Empty it last and you are the Dingus.
  • HanafudaCapture point cards and hold a yaku.
  • The Marble GameGet all four marbles home. Roll, race, and send the other colors back to start.
  • ReversiPlace a disc, flip a row, and take the board.
  • Dots & BoxesDraw a line. Close a box. Keep the chain going.
  • Word InspectorHide a secret word, ask for letters, and outscore the table.
