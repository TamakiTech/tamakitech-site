Services

RetirementEngine.io

Automated Pension Calculations for Retirement Plan Administrators and Actuarial Professionals

RetirementEngine.io is the flagship pension calculation platform developed by Tamaki Technologies LLC.

The platform supports individual and batch pension calculations using configurable plan provisions and CSV-based data processing.

Designed to simplify complex retirement calculations, RetirementEngine.io helps organizations automate repetitive processes, improve consistency, and streamline calculation testing.

Available Services

  • Custom pension plan implementations
  • Automated benefit calculation development
  • Individual and batch calculation processing
  • Independent calculation testing and validation
  • Plan-specific configuration and enhancements

Data Reconciliation & Validation

Identify discrepancies before they become costly problems.

Tamaki Technologies provides specialized data reconciliation and validation services to help organizations verify data accuracy following system conversions, software implementations, operational disruptions, and routine processing.

Services include:

  • SQL-based data reconciliation
  • Data conversion and migration validation
  • Financial and retirement data integrity testing
  • Automated exception reporting
  • Independent calculation and regression testing

Projects are delivered with clearly defined requirements, documented results, and fixed-fee pricing.

Experience Behind the Solutions

Tamaki Technologies LLC was founded by a technology and data management professional with more than 25 years of experience in pension administration, automated benefit calculations, systems analysis, software testing, and enterprise data management.

The founder holds a Master of Arts in Economics from the University of Missouri, combining advanced quantitative education with extensive hands-on experience developing and validating complex financial calculation systems.

This combination of business knowledge, analytical expertise, and software development experience allows Tamaki Technologies to deliver practical solutions tailored to each client's requirements.
